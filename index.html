01 / OUR MISSION

An encounter can become a beginning.

Founded in Vienna in January 2026, AITEA is guided by the conviction that cooperation between people of different cultures creates a foundation for mutual understanding, education, innovation and peace.

We strengthen networks and educational opportunities across sports, film, music, art and business. Rooted in European values, we champion human dignity, equality, democracy, sustainability and the protection of human rights.

We support joint projects, creative and economic summits, festivals, and collaborations in music and sports. From an Austrian perspective, Vienna serves as a gateway to the European market, connecting our cultural and business communities through industrial cooperation, knowledge exchange and future-oriented technologies.
